- example_en: a natural sentence using the word in that sense, A1 grammar (present simple,

- example_tr: what a Turkish speaker would actually say for example_en, same meaning,
  nothing added or dropped, at most 55 characters. Judge idiomatic equivalence, not

  (e.g. "Çoğulu: children." or "'house' bina, 'home' yuva/ev hissi."). Otherwise "".
